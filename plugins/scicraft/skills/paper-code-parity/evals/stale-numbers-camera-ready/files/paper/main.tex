\begin{abstract}
We present a sparse mixture adapter for vision transformers. It reaches 78.4\% top-1 accuracy on the
held-out split and is the best method on 4 of 5 benchmarks.
\end{abstract}

\section{Results}
Averaged over 3 seeds, our adapter reaches a top-1 accuracy of 0.784, against 0.776 for full fine-tuning.
Removing the router changes accuracy by $-$0.012. CLIP-L reaches 0.771.
