# TabLoom

Self-supervised pretraining × tabular data × clinical prediction

## The future of tabular learning is here

TabLoom is not just another tabular model — it's a new way to think about tables. Pretrained once · applied everywhere · zero tuning.

TabLoom wins on 9 of 12 clinical prediction tasks and is the strongest model in our benchmark. It beats the best gradient-boosted trees by a wide margin, while running 4.1 times faster at inference.

## Why it matters

- **Speed:** TabLoom is fast.
- **Accuracy:** TabLoom is accurate.
- **Simplicity:** TabLoom is simple to use.

Finally, these results speak to the power of pretraining on synthetic data. The future looks bright.

## Get started

Install with `pip install tabloom` and see the [documentation](https://example.org/tabloom/docs).
