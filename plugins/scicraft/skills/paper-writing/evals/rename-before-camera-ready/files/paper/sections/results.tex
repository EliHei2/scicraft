\section{Benchmark of retrieval against tree ensembles}\label{sec:results}
RR-Pool reaches 0.842 mean accuracy on the 40 datasets, against 0.811 for XGBoost, the highest-ranked baseline (\cref{tab:main}). Enlarging the neighbour pool from 32 to 128 rows changes accuracy by less than 0.002 (\cref{sec:neighbour-pool}).

RR-Pool (ours) & retrieval & 0.842 \\
XGBoost & tree ensemble & 0.811 \\

\end{table}
