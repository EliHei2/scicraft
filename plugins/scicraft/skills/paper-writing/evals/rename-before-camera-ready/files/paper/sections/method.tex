\section{Retrieval classifier for tabular rows}\label{sec:method}
For each query row, RR-Pool builds a neighbour pool of the $k = 32$ nearest training rows under the learned row embedding (\cref{sec:neighbour-pool}).

\subsection{Construction of the neighbour pool}\label{sec:neighbour-pool}
The neighbour pool is drawn from an index over all training rows. Rows of the query's own fold are excluded from its neighbour pool, so no test row retrieves itself.
