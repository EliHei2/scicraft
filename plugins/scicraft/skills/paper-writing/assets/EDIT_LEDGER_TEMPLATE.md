# EDIT LEDGER

The log of every change the author made to drafted text. The ledger is the log; the governing files
(the facts in GUARDRAILS.md, TERMS.txt, NARRATIVE.md, the house-style notes) are the law. A durable rule goes
into one of them too.

Classes: FACT, CLAIM, STRUCTURE, WORD, SYNTAX, CUT, HEDGE, PROCESS.

---

### E[nn] [date] [CLASS] [one-line rule]
**Trigger** [the author's words, verbatim]
**Before** [what was drafted, verbatim]
**After** [what the author wrote or required, verbatim]
**Generalizes to** [the class, and how to find its siblings, usually a grep]
**Propagated** [n found, n fixed, or pending]
**Law lives in** [the governing file that now carries the rule]
