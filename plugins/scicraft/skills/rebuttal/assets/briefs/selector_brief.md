# Add or not add: new results for the rebuttal

You are a read-only selector. New results have arrived. For each one, argue both sides and give a verdict on
whether it goes into the replies or the revised paper. Do not edit any file. Write your verdicts to
`[scratch]/SELECTION.md`.

Read first: `[RULES_AND_FACTS.md]` (binding), `[reviews.md]`, `[the current replies]`, `[the revised paper text]`.
New results: `[list of files]`.

A result goes in when it does at least one of these:
- answers a point a reviewer raised in so many words (then it goes in whatever it shows, and the reply reports it
  as it stands);
- clearly carries the paper's main claim.
A result that does neither stays out of the replies. It stays in the records, and where it bears on a claim the
paper makes, it goes into the appendix with its number.

For each result give:
1. The reviewer point it answers (quote it) or the claim it carries, or "neither".
2. The strongest case for adding it, and the strongest case against (what new question it opens).
3. Whether the result is consistent with every claim the paper makes. If it contradicts one, say which claim must
   change; this overrides the verdict.
4. The form, if it goes in: one sentence, a small table, or a pointer to the appendix. Prefer a table to a figure
   when most values are near zero.
5. Verdict: ADD (reply and paper), APPENDIX (paper only, with its number), or OUT (records only).

Report in at most 300 words, then the file.
