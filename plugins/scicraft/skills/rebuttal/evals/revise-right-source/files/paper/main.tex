\begin{abstract}
We study whether a single graph encoder can be pretrained across several molecule collections. Early results on
six tasks are promising and suggest that multi-collection pretraining helps on most of them.
\end{abstract}
\section{Introduction}
Representations for molecules are usually learned per dataset. We ask whether this is necessary, and we explore a
pretraining scheme that mixes collections. This draft reports preliminary experiments.
\section{Results}
On six tasks the encoder is competitive with the best baselines and ranks among the top methods on most tasks.
