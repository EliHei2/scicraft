\begin{abstract}
We pretrain one graph encoder on nine public molecule collections and evaluate it frozen on 14 property
prediction tasks. Among 12 compared methods, it ranks first on 9 of the 14 tasks.
\end{abstract}
\section{Introduction}
Molecular property prediction relies on representations learned from limited labeled data. Pretraining on large
unlabeled collections promises representations that transfer across tasks. Existing pretrained models are trained
on one collection at a time, which ties them to the chemistry of that collection.
\section{Results}
We compare 12 methods on 14 property prediction tasks. Our encoder ranks first on 9 of the 14 tasks, and its mean
rank is 2.1 against 4.6 for the next method.
