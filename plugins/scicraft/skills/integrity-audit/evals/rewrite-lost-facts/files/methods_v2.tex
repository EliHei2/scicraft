\section{Training}
\label{sec:training}
The encoder is a 12-layer transformer with 8 attention heads and a hidden size of 512.
We pretrain it on 1.2 million tables from a public corpus \cite{corpus2024} for 200,000 steps.
Training uses a batch size of 128 and a cosine schedule with 5,000 warmup steps.
We minimize the masked-cell loss
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L} = -\sum_{i \in M} \log p(x_i \mid x_{\setminus M}),
\end{equation}
where $M$ masks 15\% of the cells.
Numerical features are binned by quantile into 64 bins.
For fine-tuning we stop early when the validation score has not improved for 10 epochs.
