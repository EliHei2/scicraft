\section{Training}
\label{sec:training}
The encoder has 12 transformer layers with 8 attention heads and a hidden size of 512.
We pretrain on 1.2 million tables from the public corpus \cite{corpus2024} for 200,000 steps.
We use a batch size of 256, a dropout of 0.1 and a cosine schedule with 5,000 warmup steps.
The objective is the masked-cell loss of Eq.~\ref{eq:loss}:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L} = -\sum_{i \in M} \log p(x_i \mid x_{\setminus M}),
\label{eq:loss}
\end{equation}
where $M$ masks 15\% of the cells.
Numerical features are quantile-binned into 64 bins following \cite{gorishniy2022}.
Fine-tuning uses early stopping with a patience of 10 epochs on the validation split.
