\begin{abstract}
On the income benchmark our tabular model reaches 84.3\% test accuracy, ahead of the gradient-boosting baseline.
\end{abstract}
\section{Experimental setup}
We train every model with AdamW at a learning rate of $3\times10^{-4}$ for 50 epochs.
All results are test accuracy averaged over 5 seeds.
Hyperparameters are listed in \texttt{config.yaml} in the released code.
